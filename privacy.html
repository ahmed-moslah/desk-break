Breathe & Blink Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Breathe & Blink is a break-reminder app. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your settings (interval lengths, sound and notification preferences, daily goal) and your break history (how many breaks you took or skipped each day) are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • No network connections. Everything the app needs is installed with it, and it works fully offline.

Updates

The app is installed and updated through the Microsoft Store. The Store's own data handling is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, Windows shows a local reminder when a break is due. No push service or server is involved.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose Clear history, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
